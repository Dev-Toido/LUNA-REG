LUNA-REG > ABOUT

ABOUT LUNA-REG

Chandrayaan-2 Planetary Multi-Modal Image Registration Platform • SIH26166

v2.4.0-SIH26166

Autonomous Multi-Modal Planetary Registration

LUNA-REG is a specialized planetary GIS and sub-pixel image registration platform engineered for the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Space Applications Centre (SAC) problem statement SIH26166.

Orbital satellite images of the Moon captured at different orbital passes exhibit extreme variance due to differences in solar illumination angles (e.g. low-sun oblique shadows vs. high-sun nadir passes), camera optics (TMC-2 vs. OHRC), and temporal regolith modifications. LUNA-REG aligns multi-modal imagery to sub-pixel accuracy without relying on manual ground control points.

Scientific Registration Pipeline

  • Phase 1 — Keypoint Extraction: Multi-scale feature detection resilient to illumination flips and shadow inversions.
  • Phase 2 — Outlier Rejection: Progressive RANSAC with geometric homography constraint verification.
  • Phase 3 — Sub-Pixel Refinement: Mutual information maximization and local optical flow optimization.
  • Phase 4 — Cartographic Georeferencing: Projection onto the IAU 2000 Lunar Sphere (1737.4 km radius).

Target Stakeholders & Applications

  • ISRO Planetary Cartographers: Seamless orthomosaicing of Chandrayaan-2 orbital imagery.
  • Lunar Geologists: Multi-illumination structural analysis of crater terraces and central peaks.
  • Landing Hazard Analysis: High-precision co-registration for autonomous lunar hazard avoidance.
  • Hackathon & Academic Evaluators: Verification against rigorous ground-truth metrics (RMSE, inlier ratio).

Architecture & UX Standards

  • Restrained Dark Lunar GIS: Strict adherence to Obsidian, Lunar Grey, Champagne Gold, and Warm Ivory (zero generic blue tones).
  • WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA: High-contrast typography, visible focus rings, and full keyboard shortcut traversal.
  • Multi-Device Engine: Desktop 3-column GIS workspace down to mobile-first map with bottom sheet.
  • Decoupled Backend Layer: Robust API service handling real multipart/form-data with offline calibration recovery.
